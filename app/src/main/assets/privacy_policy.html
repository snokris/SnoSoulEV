SnoSoulEV Privacy Policy

SnoSoulEV is designed to work offline and does not request Android's Internet permission.

Data handled by the app

The app reads vehicle diagnostics through the Bluetooth OBD adapter you select. If you grant location permission, precise location may be used locally for the GPS display and trip-related values.

Data sharing and storage

SnoSoulEV does not transmit vehicle, Bluetooth, or location data to an online service. The current release does not offer cloud upload or public Downloads storage. Android may retain the app's private preferences and locally generated diagnostic data until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

External links

The user guide and other external links open in another app. That app and the destination website have their own privacy practices.

Project

This application is a community-maintained fork of Soul EV Spy. Review the source repository for implementation details and updates to this policy.
